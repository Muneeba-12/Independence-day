📜 CHRONICLES OF GLORY 📜

THE STORY OF 14 AUGUST 1947

How a visionary dream became an independent sovereign reality on the 27th of Ramadan 1366 AH

BABA-E-QAUM Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah

The Vision of Freedom

“There is no power on earth that can undo Pakistan.” Conceived in the intellectual foresight of Allama Muhammad Iqbal and forged by the tireless leadership of Muhammad Ali Jinnah, Pakistan emerged on the world map on 14 August 1947.

1940
🏛️

Lahore Resolution

On 23rd March 1940 at Minto Park (now Iqbal Park), the historic Pakistan Resolution was passed, demanding independent sovereign homeland for Muslims.

Minar-e-Pakistan Milestone
1947
🇵🇰

Pakistan Independence

At midnight between 14th and 15th August (27th Ramadan), the dream turned into reality. Radio Pakistan made the fateful announcement: "This is Pakistan Broadcasting Service."

Dawn of Freedom
1956
⚖️

First Constitution

Adoption of Pakistan's first formal constitution, officially proclaiming the nation as the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.

Republic Proclaimed
1973
📜

The 1973 Constitution

A landmark unanimous democratic constitution establishing the parliamentary democracy, fundamental rights, and federal framework.

Constitutional Milestone
Present
🚀

A Nation Moving Forward

A resilient nuclear power, vibrant tech hub, and dynamic youthful population marching confidently toward a prosperous, technologically advanced future.

Future of Pakistan

🏛️ NATIONAL ARCHITECTURE 🏛️

LANDMARKS OF PAKISTAN

Iconic monuments and architectural masterworks that embody our heritage and sovereign identity

Lahore
📍 Lahore, Punjab

Minar-e-Pakistan

The national monument towering at 70 meters in Iqbal Park, built to commemorate the historic Lahore Resolution passed on 23 March 1940.

Built: 1968 Height: 70m
Islamabad
📍 Islamabad, Federal Capital

Faisal Mosque

One of the world's most breathtaking modern mosques, nestled at the foot of Margalla Hills with iconic Bedouin-tent contemporary architecture.

Capacity: 300,000 Minarets: 90m
Lahore
📍 Lahore, Punjab

Badshahi Mosque

A magnificent 17th-century Mughal marvel constructed in carved red sandstone and white marble, representing Pakistan's timeless imperial heritage.

Built: 1673 Mughal Era
Islamabad
📍 Islamabad, Shakarparian

Pakistan Monument

Bloom-shaped national monument representing the four provinces and three territories of Pakistan, symbolizing unity under one grand crescent and star.

Built: 2007 Granite & Murals
Karachi
📍 Karachi, Sindh

Mazar-e-Quaid

The majestic white marble mausoleum of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah, featuring a modern parabolic arch design and serene manicured gardens.

Built: 1970 Pure White Marble
